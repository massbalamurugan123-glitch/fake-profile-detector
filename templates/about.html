How it works

The detector adds up risk points from clear, explainable rules. Good signs remove points.

Steps

  1. You enter the profile details: username, name, bio, numbers and any scam behaviour you noticed.
  2. The server checks that the input is valid.
  3. Each rule adds or removes risk points. The total is limited to 0 - 100.
  4. The score decides the verdict, and tips matching the warning signs are shown.
  5. The result is saved in the history so you can open it again.

Score meaning

  • Likely real 0 to {{ real_max }}
  • Suspicious {{ real_max + 1 }} to {{ susp_max }}
  • Likely fake {{ susp_max + 1 }} to 100

Rules that add risk

  • Asked for money, OTP or bank details (+30)
  • Spam words in bio, strange link, pressure to act fast, follow-spam pattern, new account with huge following (+15 each)
  • No profile photo, account under 1 month old (+15 each)
  • Auto-generated username, no posts, low engagement (+12 each)
  • Asking for money never scores below 40, even on an old account, because it may be hacked or cloned.
  • Smaller signals: empty bio, no mutual connections, name and username do not match

Limits you should know

  • It is a screening tool. A real person can score high and a careful fake can score low.
  • The details are typed by you, so the result is only as honest as the input.
  • It does not look at the photo itself. Check the picture yourself with a reverse image search (Google Lens or TinEye).
